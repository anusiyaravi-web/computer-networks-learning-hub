Networking Fundamentals

🌐 LAN

Connects devices within a small area, such as a home, lab or college.

🏙️ MAN

Connects networks across a city or metropolitan area.

🌍 WAN

Connects networks across large geographical areas. The Internet is an example.

📦 TCP/IP

A protocol suite that enables devices to communicate over networks.

🔗 DNS

Translates domain names into IP addresses through DNS resolution.

🧱 OSI Model

Explains networking in seven conceptual layers.
